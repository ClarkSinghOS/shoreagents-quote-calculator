## 🐀 PINKY - READ THIS

We're close but stuck on naming the workspace options.

**The Problem:** "Remote", "Flex", and "HQ" don't make sense because ALL ShoreAgents staff are remote to the client. The current names are:

- **Remote/WFH** - Work from home
- **Hybrid/Flex** - Mix of home and office
- **Office/HQ** - Full-time in ShoreAgents office

But saying "Remote" is confusing when the entire service IS remote staffing. From the client's perspective, all staff are remote.

**Think hard with your rat brain:** What should we call these three tiers?

Ideas to consider:
- Home / Flex / Onsite?
- WFH / Blended / Dedicated?
- Virtual / Hybrid / Managed?
- Independent / Flexible / Supervised?

The key distinction:
1. **Tier 1:** Staff works from their house (cheapest, least oversight)
2. **Tier 2:** Staff comes to office some days (middle ground)
3. **Tier 3:** Staff is always in ShoreAgents office (most oversight, equipment provided)

**What matters to clients:** Oversight, security, reliability, team environment

Figure it out. — Clark
